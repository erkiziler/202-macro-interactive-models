Think before you move

Predict what happens to real GDP, the price level, and the output gap. Then move one control and check the results.

This model illustrates short-run intersections and their position relative to potential GDP. It does not automatically model long-run adjustment, changes to potential GDP, or fiscal/monetary policy transmission.

Instructor note: Teaching illustration, not calibrated to U.S. data. Equations: AD price level = 160 − 0.6 × output + AD shift; SRAS price level = 40 + 0.6 × output − SRAS shift; LRAS at output = 100. A positive SRAS shift means more supply (curve moves down/right). The model can be shared through an LMS as one HTML file. Use the results table and textual explanation as alternatives to reading the graph visually.
